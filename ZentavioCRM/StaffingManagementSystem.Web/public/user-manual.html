How to use this manual

This document has two audiences and both can read it straight through.

  • End users get a plain-language walk-through of every screen: what it's for, what each field means, and the steps to complete common tasks.
  • QA testers get the same walk-through plus two extra layers highlighted throughout in colored boxes: orange QA Focus boxes calling out specific things worth testing (edge cases, mismatches between what the screen offers and what the server actually allows), and red Known Gap boxes documenting things that are not yet built or behave differently than you might expect — these are not bugs to re-report, they're documented current behavior, useful as a checklist when triaging new findings.

Section 14, Permissions Reference lists every permission code in the system. Section 16, Known Limitations & Test Focus Summary collects every QA/Gap box in the manual into one master checklist.

1. System Overview

ZentavioCRM is a sales-pipeline CRM covering the full customer-acquisition lifecycle: a Lead comes in, gets qualified, converts into a Customer and/or an Opportunity, an Opportunity produces one or more Quotations, an accepted Quotation becomes a Sales Order, and a Sales Order tracks delivery until complete. Around this pipeline sit administrative modules (Users, Roles, Departments, Territories) and supporting features (Activities, Documents, Notifications, Audit History).

Access to every module is controlled by permissions assigned to a user's Role. On top of that, a Role also carries a Visibility Scope (Own / Team / All) that controls which records a user can see within Leads, Customers and Opportunities — see section 12 for full details, since this is one of the most important behaviors to understand and to test.

ConceptWhat it means here
LeadAn unqualified prospect captured from a source (website, referral, campaign, etc.)
CustomerA qualified account — a company or individual with a full record of contacts, addresses, and history
OpportunityA specific, tracked deal against a Customer, moving through sales stages
QuotationA versioned, priced proposal document tied to one Opportunity
Sales OrderThe confirmed order created once a Quotation is accepted; tracks delivery

2. Getting Started

2.1 Logging in

Go to the login screen and enter your email address and password (minimum 8 characters). Check Remember Me to stay signed in on this device between browser sessions; leave it unchecked and your session ends when you close the browser tab.

Known Gap — Session expiry Your sign-in session (access token) expires after 15 minutes. There is currently no automatic renewal and no "your session expired, please log in again" message — API calls will simply start failing. If the app seems to stop responding to actions, refresh the page and log in again.
Known Gap — Forgot Password / SSO The Forgot Password? link is shown on the login screen but is not functional yet. There is also no password-reset or password-change feature anywhere in the app once an account is created — contact your administrator if you need your password changed.

2.2 The Dashboard

The Dashboard (/dashboard) is the landing page after login and is visible to every signed-in user regardless of permissions. It shows: Open Leads, Active Customers, Leads Converted This Month, total Pipeline Value, Open Opportunities, Win Rate percentage, and a Pipeline by Stage breakdown (count and value per Opportunity stage).

Behavior Dashboard counts respect the viewer's Visibility Scope, the same as the Leads/Customers/Opportunities list screens. A user restricted to "Own" or "Team" sees totals limited to the records they're allowed to see, not company-wide figures — so the numbers here always match what that user can actually open elsewhere in the app.

2.3 Navigation

The left sidebar lists every module the signed-in user has permission to view; a module the user lacks permission for simply does not appear as a menu item (it is not just visually disabled — the underlying pages and API calls are also blocked, see section 12).

3. My Profile

Route: /profile · reached via your name/avatar in the top bar

3.1 Profile Photo

  1. Open My Profile and click Upload Photo (or Change Photo if you already have one).
  2. Choose an image file. Accepted types: PNG, JPEG, GIF, up to 5 MB.
  3. The photo appears immediately across the app (top bar, Users list, avatars anywhere your name appears).
  4. Use Remove to delete your photo and fall back to your initials.

You can manage your own photo at any time. An administrator holding the Users.Manage permission can also add/remove a photo for any other user from that user's edit screen.

Behavior The file-type restriction is enforced on the server against the file's actual bytes (its signature), not the filename or the browser-supplied Content-Type. A renamed non-image file (e.g. a .html file saved with a .png extension) is rejected even though the browser would otherwise report it as image/png.

3.2 Out-of-Office / Delegation

Use this to temporarily hand off your assigned records and reminders to a colleague while you're away.

  1. On My Profile, scroll to Out of Office / Delegation.
  2. Choose a Delegate To colleague, a Start Date and End Date, and optional notes.
  3. Click Add. The delegation appears in the list below with an Active/Not Active badge.
  4. Click Cancel on any delegation you created to end it early.

While a delegation is active: your delegate can see your assigned Leads, Customers and Opportunities exactly as if they owned them — regardless of the delegate's own Visibility Scope — and your delegate also receives your due-date, follow-up, birthday and anniversary reminder notifications, in addition to you (you are not muted while delegating).

Business rule You cannot delegate to yourself, and End Date must be on or after Start Date. Only the person who created a delegation can cancel it — cancelling someone else's delegation (e.g. by guessing an ID) returns a generic "not found" response.

4. Leads Own / Team / All scoped

Route: /leads · Permissions: Leads.View Leads.Create Leads.Edit Leads.Delete Leads.Assign Leads.Convert

A Lead is an unqualified prospect. It carries a lead number (auto-generated, e.g. LEAD-000123), company and contact details, source and campaign/UTM tracking, an expected value/budget/timeline, a status, and a calculated Lead Score.

4.1 Fields

FieldNotes
Company Name / Contact NameRequired
Email / MobileOptional; email is format-validated
IndustryOptional free text
SourceWebsite, Landing Page, Referral, Exhibition, WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, Email Campaign, Google Ads, Manual Entry, API Integration
CampaignFree-text campaign label
UTM Source / Medium / Campaign / Term / ContentStructured tracking parameters, separate from the Campaign label
Budget / Expected ValueNumeric, optional
TimelineFree text (e.g. "this quarter")
Territory (legacy free text)Older free-text field, kept for backward compatibility with CSV import/export
Territory (structured)Optional link to a Territory record — the two Territory fields are independent; a lead can have either, both, or neither
StatusSee status flow below
Next Follow-Up DateDrives the follow-up reminder — see section 13
Lead Score0–100, calculated automatically (see below), read-only

4.2 Status flow

New → Assigned → Contacted → (Qualified or Nurturing) → Proposal Sent → (Converted, Lost, or Junk).

QA Focus — status transitions The screen only offers a curated set of "next status" buttons, but the server accepts almost any status change (except leaving a terminal status). A direct API call can jump between non-adjacent statuses that the UI never offers — e.g. Nurturing straight back to New. Test both through the UI and via direct API calls if you have that access.
Business rule A Reason is required when moving a Lead to Lost or Junk. Once a Lead is Converted, Lost, or Junk it becomes read-only — it cannot be edited, reassigned, or have its status changed again, even though the error message references "reopening" a lead, which is not an available action anywhere in the app.

4.3 Lead Score

Recalculated automatically every time a lead is saved: up to +15 for having an email, +15 for a mobile number, +10 for industry filled in, +10 for being assigned, up to +25 for a high expected value, +20 for a Referral source (+10 for several other sources), and +5 if the Timeline text suggests urgency. Capped at 100. This is a simple rule-based score, not AI-driven (an "AI Score" field exists in the data model for a future scoring engine but is not populated by anything today).

4.4 Everyday actions

  1. Create a Lead — Leads.Create. As you enter Email or Mobile, the form checks for duplicates against existing Leads and Customers and shows a dismissible warning with links to the possible matches — this is informational only and does not block saving.
  2. Assign a Lead — Leads.Assign. Sets the owner and automatically advances status from New to Assigned; the new owner gets a notification.
  3. Convert to Customer — Leads.Convert. Creates a new Customer record from the lead's company details and marks the lead Converted.
  4. Convert to Opportunity — Leads.Convert. Creates (or reuses, if already converted) the Customer and a new Opportunity in one step, carrying across the expected value.
  5. Delete a Lead — Leads.Delete. Available on the Lead detail screen, with a confirmation prompt. This permanently removes the lead (it is not a soft-delete).

5. Opportunities Own / Team / All scoped

Route: /opportunities · Permissions: Opportunities.View Opportunities.Create Opportunities.Edit Opportunities.Delete Opportunities.Assign

An Opportunity is a tracked deal against a specific Customer. It has line items (products/quantities/prices), a buying-committee contact list, a stage, and a value that is auto-computed from its line items whenever any exist.

5.1 Fields

FieldNotes
NameRequired
CustomerRequired — picking a customer also filters the buying-committee contact list to that customer's own contacts
ValueAuto-calculated as the sum of line items once any line item is added; otherwise entered manually
CurrencyDefaults from the customer's currency, editable afterward
Probability0–100%
ProductsFree-text summary, independent of the structured line items
CompetitorsFree text
Expected Close Date 
StageSee stage flow below
Next Step / Next Step DateThe single next action to move the deal forward
Line ItemsProduct name, quantity, unit price, discount % — each computes a line total
Buying CommitteeLinks existing contacts from the same customer, each tagged with a role: Champion, Economic Buyer, Blocker, Influencer, Decision Maker, Technical Evaluator, Other

5.2 Stage flow

Qualification → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Verbal Commit → (Closed Won or Closed Lost).

QA Focus — stage transitions As with Leads, the UI presents a linear pipeline, but the server does not enforce that order — only that a Closed Won/Closed Lost opportunity cannot change again, and that Closed Lost requires a reason. A direct API call can jump from Qualification straight to Negotiation, skipping stages. Good candidate for a negative test case.

Reaching Closed Won or Closed Lost notifies both the assignee and the creator.

5.3 Everyday actions

  1. Create an Opportunity directly, or via Convert to Opportunity from a Lead.
  2. Assign — Opportunities.Assign; blocked once the opportunity is closed.
  3. Create Quotation button on the Opportunity detail page (requires Quotations.Create) jumps straight to a pre-linked new Quotation.
Known Gap No Delete button in the UI despite a working API endpoint. Deleting an Opportunity that already has a Quotation will fail at the database level with a generic error rather than a friendly message, because the service does not pre-check for dependent Quotations.

6. Quotations Not visibility-scoped

Route: /quotations · Permissions: Quotations.View Quotations.Create Quotations.Edit Quotations.Delete Quotations.Assign

A Quotation is a versioned, priced proposal tied to exactly one Opportunity. Totals (subtotal, tax, grand total) are computed automatically from its line items, which additionally carry a tax percentage (unlike Opportunity line items).

Important behavior Unlike Leads, Customers and Opportunities, Quotations are not subject to Own/Team/All Visibility Scope. Anyone holding Quotations.View can see every quotation in the system regardless of who created or is assigned to it.

6.1 Status flow (strictly enforced)

Draft → Sent → (Accepted, Rejected, or Expired). Unlike Leads/Opportunities, this transition list is fully enforced on the server — you cannot skip Draft straight to Accepted, and no transition is possible once Accepted/Rejected/Expired.

6.2 Everyday actions

  1. Create — pick an Opportunity (locked once saved) and add at least one line item.
  2. Edit — only possible while the quotation is still Draft.
  3. New Version — clones the current line items into a fresh quotation at the next version number, always starting at Draft again. Available regardless of the current quotation's status, so you can spin up v2 from an Accepted, Rejected, or Expired v1.
  4. Convert to Sales Order — appears once Status = Accepted and no sales order exists yet for it; requires SalesOrders.Create.
  5. Delete — only possible while Draft (no UI button currently, API only).

Reaching Accepted notifies the assignee/creator that the quotation is ready to convert.

7. Sales Orders Not visibility-scoped

Route: /sales-orders · Permissions: SalesOrders.View SalesOrders.Create SalesOrders.Edit SalesOrders.Assign

A Sales Order is created only by converting an Accepted Quotation — there is no standalone "New Sales Order" screen. Its pricing (subtotal/tax/total, line items) is a permanent snapshot taken at conversion time; later changes to the source quotation never retroactively change an already-created order. One Quotation can produce at most one Sales Order.

7.1 Status flow

Draft/Confirmed (set automatically at creation) → Partially Delivered → Delivered, or Cancelled at any point before full delivery. Partially Delivered and Delivered are calculated automatically from recorded deliveries — there is no manual "set status" action for those two.

7.2 Everyday actions

  1. Record Delivery — on the order detail page, enter delivered quantity per line item (capped at the remaining undelivered amount). Saving recalculates the order's status automatically.
  2. Cancel — available any time before the order is fully delivered.
  3. Assign, and Update notes/expected delivery date (blocked once cancelled).
Known Gap A SalesOrders.Delete permission exists and can be granted to a Role, but there is no delete endpoint or button anywhere — granting it currently has no effect.

8. Customers Own / Team / All scoped

Route: /customers · Permissions: Customers.View Customers.Create Customers.Edit Customers.Delete

The Customer record ("Customer Master") is the central account record: legal/display name, type, industry, financials, tags, and health status, plus unlimited child Contacts and Addresses.

8.1 Key fields

FieldNotes
Customer NumberAuto-generated (e.g. CUST-000123)
TypeProspect, Individual, Business, Vendor, Partner, Supplier, Distributor, Dealer, Franchise, Consultant
Legal Name / Display NameLegal Name is required; Display Name defaults to Legal Name if left blank

Health StatusHot / Warm / Cold / At Risk — a distinct concept from Rating, describing relationship health
TagsFree-text, comma-separated
Acquisition SourceFilled automatically when converted from a Lead
Credit Limit / Payment Terms / CurrencyFinancial terms

8.2 Contacts (unlimited per customer)

Name, designation, department, email, mobile, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, whether they are the primary contact and/or a decision maker, preferred contact method, and Date of Birth / Anniversary Date, which drive the reminder notifications described in section 13.

8.3 Addresses (unlimited per customer)

Type (Billing, Shipping, Registered Office, Branch Office, Warehouse, Site), address lines, city/state/country/postal code, and a primary flag.

Known Gap — saving replaces child records Every time a Customer is saved, its entire Contacts list and entire Addresses list are replaced, not diffed. Removing a contact/address row and saving permanently deletes that row (including its history); it is not a soft-remove. Be deliberate when editing these lists.

8.4 Everyday actions

  1. Search/filter by name, number, email, assigned owner, active status.
  2. Export to CSV and Import from CSV (10 MB limit) — note contacts and addresses are not part of the CSV import; add them afterward on the edit screen.
  3. Documents panel — attach files to the customer record.
  4. History panel — a plain-English audit trail of changes.
Known Gap No Delete button in the UI. Deleting a Customer that has any Opportunity will currently fail with an unhandled database error rather than a friendly message, because the delete does not pre-check for dependent Opportunities.

9. Departments

Route: /departments · Permissions: Departments.View Departments.Manage

A simple, optionally hierarchical (parent/child) organizational grouping. Every user can optionally belong to one department. Department membership is what Team Visibility Scope uses to decide who counts as "your team" — see section 12.

  1. Create/Edit — name (must be unique) and an optional parent department.
  2. Delete — blocked if any user is currently assigned to the department; reassign or remove those users first.

The list shows a live user count and active/inactive status per department.

10. Territories

Route: /territories · Permissions: Territories.View Territories.Manage

A structured, optionally hierarchical territory list, assignable to both Users and Leads. This is a newer, structured replacement direction for the older free-text Territory field on Leads — both fields exist side by side today (see section 4) and neither is required.

Important — not the same as Visibility Scope Territory is purely an organizational/reporting classification. It does not feed into the Own/Team/All record-visibility engine at all — "Team" scope is based on Department membership, not Territory. Don't confuse the two when testing access control.
  1. Create/Edit — name (unique) and optional parent territory.
  2. Delete — blocked if any User or any Lead currently references the territory.

The list shows a live user count and live lead count per territory, plus active/inactive status.

Every login account: employee code, name, email, mobile, role, department, reporting manager, territory, and profile photo.

FieldNotes

A deactivated user cannot log in (a deliberately vague "invalid email or password" message is shown, rather than revealing the account exists but is inactive).

The user's own profile photo is managed from My Profile, or by an administrator from this Users screen.

12. Roles, Permissions & Visibility Scope

Route: /roles · Permissions: Roles.View Roles.Manage

A Role bundles a set of module permissions plus one Visibility Scope, and is assigned to one or more Users. This is the single mechanism controlling both what a user can do and which records they can see.

12.1 Managing Roles

  1. Create/Edit — name (unique), description, Visibility Scope, and a checkbox grid of every permission in the system grouped by module.
  2. Saving a role's permissions replaces the full permission set — there's no incremental add/remove, the form always submits everything currently checked.
  3. Delete — blocked if any user currently has that role.
System roles Built-in roles (such as Admin) are marked as System Roles and cannot be renamed, edited, or deleted — the Edit/Delete controls are hidden for them in the UI, and the API independently rejects any attempt to modify or delete a system role even via a direct call.

12.2 Visibility Scope — Own / Team / All

This setting on the Role controls which Leads, Customers, and Opportunities a user can see and act on. It does not affect Quotations, Sales Orders, Departments, Territories, Users, or Roles — those modules are governed by permissions alone.

ScopeWhat the user can see
OwnOnly records assigned to them, or — if unassigned — records they created
TeamEverything in Own, plus records assigned to or created by anyone else in the same Department. A user with no department behaves the same as Own.
AllEvery record in the module, company-wide

An active Delegation is layered on top of all three scopes: a delegate can see the delegator's records regardless of the delegate's own scope.

QA Focus — how to test scope
  • Trying to open a record outside your scope (e.g. by URL/ID) returns the same "not found" response as a genuinely non-existent record — it never says "forbidden." This is deliberate (it avoids confirming a record exists to someone who shouldn't see it), but means QA should not assume a 404-style response equals "the ID was wrong."
  • List screens (search results, counts, pagination) are filtered server-side per scope; single-record screens (open/edit/delete/assign/status-change) are checked independently after fetching the record. Test both paths — a bug could exist in one but not the other.
  • If a Role can't be resolved at all for some reason, the system defaults that user to Own scope (fail-closed, not fail-open) — the safer default.

13. Notifications & Reminders

The bell icon in the top bar shows a live unread count and a dropdown of recent notifications. Clicking an unread notification marks it read and, where applicable, opens the related record.

13.1 What generates a notification

  • An overdue Activity assigned to you that hasn't been completed yet.
  • A Lead assigned to you whose Next Follow-Up Date has arrived.
  • A Customer contact's birthday, for contacts belonging to a customer assigned to you.
  • A Customer contact's anniversary date, same ownership rule as above.
  • Being assigned a Lead or Opportunity, and a Quotation reaching Accepted, or a Sales Order becoming fully Delivered.
  • If someone is currently delegating to you, you additionally receive their due-date/follow-up/birthday/anniversary notifications on their behalf (see section 3.2) — they still receive their own copy too.

Each reminder fires once per due item (it is stamped internally so it won't repeat), and a follow-up date change on a Lead resets that stamp so it can fire again against the new date.

Known Gap — polling only, no push There is no background job/scheduler in this build. Reminders are only checked and generated at the moment a signed-in user's browser polls for notifications, which happens automatically roughly every 30 seconds while the app is open. If nobody who owns (or is delegated) the overdue item is logged in, the reminder simply does not get created until the next time someone opens the app — it is not silently missed forever, but it is also not real-time or guaranteed while no one is online.

14. Activities, Documents & History

These three panels appear embedded on Lead, Opportunity, and/or Customer detail screens rather than as their own top-level menu items. They are available to any signed-in user — there is no dedicated permission gate on them today.

14.1 Activities

A timeline of calls, emails, meetings, tasks, notes, visits, WhatsApp messages, and SMS logged against a record, each with a subject, description, due date, and assignee.

Recurrence Setting a recurrence rule (Daily/Weekly/Monthly) and a count (2–52) generates all occurrences immediately as separate Activity rows — there is no background process creating future occurrences over time, since none exists in this build.

14.2 Documents

Generic file attachments (up to 10 MB) against a Customer or Opportunity record, stored directly in the database (no external cloud storage integration).

14.3 History / Audit Log

A plain-English list of what happened to a record — created, updated, deleted, status/stage changed, assigned, converted, cancelled, delivery recorded — with who and when. It records that something changed and in what category, but not a field-by-field before/after diff.

17. Known Limitations & Test Focus Summary

A single checklist gathering every orange/red callout in this manual, for quick reference during test planning.

  • Access tokens expire after 15 minutes with no refresh and no "please log in again" message — the app just starts failing silently.
  • Forgot Password is visible but non-functional; there is no password reset/change flow anywhere post-creation.
  • Lead status and Opportunity stage transitions are enforced loosely on the server (only terminal-state and reason-required rules) even though the UI presents a stricter linear flow — test both the UI paths and out-of-sequence transitions directly.
  • Visibility Scope (Own/Team/All) applies only to Leads, Customers, Opportunities, and the Dashboard's summary counts — not to Quotations or Sales Orders.
  • Out-of-scope records return a generic "not found," never "forbidden" — don't conflate the two when triaging.
  • Deleting a Customer with existing Opportunities, or an Opportunity with existing Quotations, or a Quotation with an existing Sales Order, is expected to fail ungracefully (a raw database error) rather than a friendly validation message, since those delete operations don't pre-check dependents.
  • Customers, Opportunities and Quotations each have a working Delete API but no Delete button in the UI (Leads now has one, on the detail screen). Sales Orders have a Delete permission with no feature behind it at all.
  • Editing a Customer or an Opportunity fully replaces their child collections (Contacts/Addresses, or Line Items/Buying Committee) — removing a row and saving is a permanent delete of that row.
  • Record numbers (LEAD-, CUST-, OPP-, QUO-, SO-) are based on current row counts, not a guaranteed sequence — deleting and recreating records can produce number collisions.
  • Reminders (due activities, follow-ups, birthdays, anniversaries) only get generated when a relevant user's browser polls the app (roughly every 30 seconds while logged in) — there is no background scheduler, so nothing fires while no relevant user is online.
  • Recurring Activities generate every occurrence immediately at creation time rather than progressively over time.
  • Territory is unrelated to Visibility Scope — "Team" scope is based on Department, not Territory.
